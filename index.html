Same moment, two takes

In a close-up, less reads as more.

On stage you show the audience what you feel. On camera the lens is a few feet from your face and picks up every thought, so pushing it reads as “acting”.

The Camera Actor’s Toolkit trains the take on the right: where to look, what to do with your breath and your silences, and how to let a thought land without forcing it.
