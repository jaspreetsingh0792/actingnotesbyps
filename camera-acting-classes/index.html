Camera acting classes in Gurgaon & Delhi NCR

Most acting classes train you for the stage. These are built for the camera: self-tapes, auditions, web series and close-ups, where the lens is a few feet from your face and picks up every thought. You perform on camera, we watch it back together, and you get specific notes on what to change.

What you’ll work on

  • Size for the frame: how big to play a wide shot, a mid shot and an extreme close-up.
  • Thought, not expression: why a take looks “fake” and how to keep the thought alive without pushing it onto your face.
  • Eyeline and stillness: where to look, when to blink, and how to listen on camera.
  • Self-tape craft: framing, light and sound with just a phone, and the checklist casting teams care about.
  • Getting camera-ready: a short face and breath release to drop tension before every take.

Common questions

Are the classes for beginners?

Yes. Sessions start from how the camera sees you. Experienced and theatre actors get just as much from the playback and personal notes.

Where are the classes held?

Workshops run at White and Grey Studio, Sector 31, Faridabad, a five-minute walk from Mewala Maharajpur metro on the Violet Line. Private coaching is available in Gurgaon, in Faridabad or online.

Do I need to prepare a monologue?

No. Short scenes are given on the day. If you have an audition or self-tape coming up, bring the script and we can work on it.

How is camera acting different from theatre acting?

On stage you project to the back row. On camera the lens is a few feet from your face, so the thought has to stay at full strength while the outward expression gets smaller. Eyeline, stillness and listening matter far more on screen.
