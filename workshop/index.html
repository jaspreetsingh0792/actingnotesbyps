What we’ll do over 2 days

Day 1 is exercises that make you real on camera, for beginners and experienced actors alike. Day 2 is your audition: we shoot it, correct it and shoot it again until it’s ready to send to casting.

Day 1 · Saturday 14 Nov · Exercises that work on camera

11:00Warm-up. Body, breath and focus.
11:15Repetition exercise. In pairs, on camera: real listening, so your reactions come from the other actor, not from planning.
11:55Imagination exercise. Building the place and the person you’re talking to, so the scene is real for you before you say a word.
12:30Understanding the script. Who you are, what you want, what’s in the way and what just happened, worked out on a real scene.
1:10Staying in the scene. Keeping the thought alive through the whole take, including pauses and when you’re not speaking.
1:45Your audition scene. Each actor gets their scene to prepare for Day 2.

Day 2 · Sunday 15 Nov · Your audition, made perfect

11:00Warm-up.
11:15Shoot your audition. Each actor records their scene on a lit set, with a proper slate.
12:15Watch back and corrections. Personal notes from me on your take.
1:00Final takes. Shoot again with the corrections until it’s right.
1:45Wrap. Your best take is edited and sent to you within 48 hours, ready for casting.
